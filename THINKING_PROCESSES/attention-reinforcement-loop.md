# Attention and reactivation loop

**Status:** Candidate process model; provisional, not empirically validated.

## Process

`representation activated → attention allocated → elaboration / association → accessibility or association changes → probability of future activation changes`

This process is a working model for investigating how revisiting a mental representation may affect its later influence. It is not necessarily a one-way strengthening loop. Depending on context, reactivation can strengthen, weaken, update, or habituate a response.

## Inputs and moderators

- Visit frequency (V)
- Attentional dose (D)
- Elaboration (E)
- Affective context (A)
- Associative connectivity (C)
- Prior salience and learning
- Spacing and time interval
- Competing or corrective representations

## Observable outputs

Potential outputs include retrieval accessibility, attentional capture, interpretation, evaluation, emotional response, and behaviour. These outcomes must be measured separately; one cannot be assumed from another.

## Causal caution

A highly salient representation may be revisited more often, creating reverse causality. A test should measure baseline salience and distinguish repeated exposure from attention and elaboration.

## Failure conditions

- Exposure without attention
- Habituation
- Contradictory learning or updating
- Familiarity/confidence mistaken for accuracy
